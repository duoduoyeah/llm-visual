# Reference example: prompt + output

The canonical sample we use when redesigning the output visualization.
Short prompt, 5 output paragraphs, each at most 2 sentences.

## Prompt

My sourdough starter smells like nail polish remover. Is it dead?

## Output

It's not dead — that acetone smell is just a hungry starter. When the yeast burns through all the available flour, it starts producing acetone as a byproduct.

You'll usually see a layer of dark liquid on top too, which bakers call hooch. That's alcohol, and it's another sign the culture has been sitting too long between feedings.

The fix is simple: pour off the hooch, discard all but a spoonful of starter, and feed it fresh flour and water. Do this twice a day for two or three days.

By the second day the smell should shift from solvent to something closer to yogurt or beer. That tangy, slightly sweet smell is what a healthy starter is supposed to smell like.

If you're not baking often, keep it in the fridge and feed it once a week instead. It'll go dormant rather than starving, and you can wake it up with a couple of room-temperature feedings before you need it.
